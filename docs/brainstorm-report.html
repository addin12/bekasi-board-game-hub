Board Game Hub · Brainstorm v1 · 18 Nov 2026

A hub built around the player card

The core idea is a player card for every member, and a site that filters those cards. You can find people who love the same games, people who own or want to play the game you want, and people selling the game you're after. Sessions, places, the feed and badges all feed into that one database.

Your goals

Meet like-minded players

Match on shared favorites and recently loved games.

Connect owners with players

People who own a game meet people who want to play it, and the other way round.

Build a list of places to play

Every logged session can tag a place, so the places list grows by itself.

Gamify the session

Badges and stats come from logged sessions, not from posting a lot.

Your sketches
First finding

Reroll already does most of the feature list

Reroll.id already has find players, play logs with winners and scores, a marketplace with auctions, places, events, discussions and a game database, all for Indonesia. If Board Game Hub tries to be the same thing, it competes head-on with a bigger site.

Where we're different: Reroll is built around listings and the catalog. We're built around people. The player card is the main object, matching is the main action, and the hub starts with your WhatsApp group (verified members) instead of all of Indonesia. Public pages bring in new people.

Key flows

How each feature works

Find (the core)

  • Like-minded: ranked by how many favorite and hot games you share.
  • To play with: people who want the same game, plus people who own a game you want to play.
  • Buy/sell: their selling list matched against your looking-to-buy list, and the other way round.
  • Filters: game, district, verified only. Results show player cards.

Game page

Click any game and see tabs for Owns · Loves · Wants to play · Selling · Looking to buy, plus recent sessions and the places it was played. Game details come from BGG and are cached.

Session scheduler

  1. The host picks the game, time, place and number of seats, and whether it's open to anyone or invite only.
  2. The host invites people from Find. Others tap “Join.”
  3. After the session, the host gets a prompt: who played, the winner (optional), a photo.
  4. A post is written automatically: “X played Anu with Y and Z at Place.”

Trade (the selling flow)

  1. The seller lists a game for sale. People with it on their looking-to-buy list get notified.
  2. A buyer taps “Interested,” and the seller sees their WhatsApp contact.
  3. The deal happens outside the site. The app never handles money.
  4. The seller marks it sold to @buyer and the buyer confirms. Then a post goes up automatically: “M sold Inu to X.”

Places

Members add public venues: cafés, stores, community spaces. A session tags a place, so each place gets a session count and a “games played here” list. Places link out to Google Maps. A private home shows only the district.

Verified member badge

Settings has a “Member passcode” field. An admin sets the code and changes it from time to time. The right code gives a verified badge. We store only a hash of the code, limit wrong attempts, and the admin can remove the badge.

Push notifications

You're invited to a session. Someone joins your session. You're tagged in a post. A game for sale matches your looking-to-buy list. A session reminder 2 hours before. Each one has its own on/off switch.

iPhone only gets push after the site is added to the home screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). The in-app bell always works.

Feed

A simple feed like Threads: a photo or text post, likes, comments and @tags. Session reports and sales show up in the same feed. No reposts and no algorithm. Newest first, with a filter for “people near me.”

Design direction

Dark charcoal satin, colors reserved for categories

Look

  • Charcoal Satin (a slow, silky moving background) only on the landing hero and the top of the player card. Everything else stays still for readability.
  • One accent color, satin silver, for main buttons.
  • Your 4 category colors (favorites, hotness, want to play, trade) only on icons and labels, never on buttons.
  • Your UI rules apply: 16px or larger text, labels above fields, 44px tap targets, and designed for phones first.

Which motion goes where

From the 32 free transitions.dev recipes now saved in design-kit/transitions:

  • like-button for post likes
  • notification-badge for the bell
  • avatar-group-hover for the people in a session
  • card-tilt for the player card
  • tabs-sliding for the game page tabs
  • plus-menu-morph for the new post, session or listing button
  • success-check and toast for joining a session
  • skeleton-reveal while the feed loads
  • number-pop-in for badge counts

Motion (motion.dev) covers page transitions and layout animations. Everything respects reduced-motion settings.

Build order

Three phases

Phase 1

Cards and Find

Sign-in, player card editor, BGG game search and collection import, game page showing who owns, loves, wants and sells it, Find with filters, public pages, share button, member passcode.

Phase 2

Sessions, places, feed

Session scheduler with invite and join, places, automatic session posts, photo posts with likes, comments and tags, push notifications.

Phase 3

Trade and badges

Selling and looking-to-buy matching, sold-and-confirmed flow, badges, hotness suggestions from sessions, yearly recap, Indonesian language.
