# Anti-slop audit 001 (2026-11-18)

## Scope and method

- Scope: the sandbox design at commit a27235b. It covers home, sign-in, Find, card editor, player card, game page, settings, and admin.
- Method: the Anti-Slop rules R-01 to R-40 and the Delivery Gate (github.com/miqdadbadjuber/Anti-Slop). Also the `design-elevation` interrogation checklist from design-skills (github.com/cuellarfr/design-skills).
- Owner's verdict before the audit: "really so generic and too much use of a template".
- Approval: the owner asked us to fix all findings ("de-slop it all").

## Root cause

We read "playful" as a stock trend look. That look has neo-brutalist stickers, a dot grid, tilted pills, a marker highlight, and trendy fonts. We did not use the owner's own design language. That language already exists in `brainstorm/player-card.png`.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | R-23 | We invented a logo (a dice icon in a tilted yellow square). Nobody asked for it. | Use a text wordmark until the community has a logo. |
| 2 | HIGH | R-27 | No loading state while a page or filter loads. Find filters, tabs, and card saves look frozen. | Show a thin progress bar during navigation. |
| 3 | HIGH | R-40 | Game covers and avatars have no width and height. | Add dimensions. |
| 4 | HIGH | R-39, R-32 | Card links and list rows show hover. They use the browser default for keyboard focus. | Use one visible focus ring for every focusable element. |
| 5 | HIGH | R-33 | A string-replacement script applied the previous restyle. | Write this pass directly in the source. |
| 6 | HIGH | R-37 | There was no written design direction. We did not use the owner's sketch as the source. | Write `DESIGN.md` from the sketch. |
| 7 | MEDIUM | R-07 | The dot-grid page background has no real purpose. | Remove it. |
| 8 | MEDIUM | R-12 | A hard offset shadow is on every surface (cards, buttons, inputs, tabs, chips). | Keep the shadow on the player card only. It is the one physical object. |
| 9 | MEDIUM | R-04 | Thin-stroke icons serve as decoration (feature tiles, logo, Find modes). | Keep only the 4 category icons from the sketch and the map pin. Fill them as the sketch does. |
| 10 | MEDIUM | R-09 | An eyebrow pill above the H1 repeats "Bekasi". | Remove it. |
| 11 | MEDIUM | R-14 | Three identical feature cards have icon tiles. | Replace them with the card anatomy (see 15). |
| 12 | MEDIUM | R-06 | We picked the typefaces (Bricolage Grotesque and Nunito) as a trend. | Use Montserrat, the face in the owner's sketch. |
| 13 | MEDIUM | R-19, R-01 | Decorative tilts (pill, feature cards) and a marker highlight under "table". | Remove them. Keep one tilt on the hero card, like a card dropped on the table. |
| 14 | MEDIUM | R-01 | A dice-pip pattern is on the card header. | Use a plain orange header like the sketch. |
| 15 | LOW | R-05 | Home is a hero, 3 cards, and a grid. This is a template. | Show a hero with the card. Then show the card anatomy with the numbered markers from the sketch. Then show the newest real members. |
| 16 | LOW | R-29 | The palette has yellow, orange, lavender, cream, and 4 category colors. | Use orange as the brand color, plus ink and warm neutrals. Use category colors on card labels only. |
| 17 | LOW | R-20 | If you swap the name, the site looks like any neo-brutalist site. | Use identity motifs from the sketch: round game tokens, numbered black markers, and an orange card header. |
| 18 | LOW | R-11 | Every element uses `rounded-xl`. | Use a radius system: 20px for cards, 10px for controls, round for tokens. |
| 19 | LOW | R-05 | The Find mode switch looks like three feature cards. | Use a tab bar with a clear current tab. |
| 20 | LOW | R-20 | The card drifts from the sketch (square covers, dashed rules). | Overlap the round favorites. Use solid rules. Put the trade arrows on the token corner. |

The follow-up report, `audit-001-fix-report.md`, records the changes and the Delivery Gate.
